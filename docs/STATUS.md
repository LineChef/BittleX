## What has been exercised on the real robot

- **Hard-floor walking (V2.1):** six clean 10-cycle runs, ~0.118 m/s, steady; drifts right and rolls ~±6° ([`rl/real-walk-log.md`](rl/real-walk-log.md)).
- **Firmware step gait (`vtF`):** steady after the IMU calibration. **Pi serial link, 5 Hz IMU, fall guard, camera feed:** working.
- **Voice benchmark on the Pi (2026-10-03):** RAM fits, thermals fine, but Piper and Vosk both run slower than real time — numbers in [`guides/pi-bring-up.md`](guides/pi-bring-up.md) §8. Mic capture works; the Claude round-trip from the Pi is not measured yet.
- **Voice boot service (2026-10-03):** `g2-voice` is installed and enabled on the Pi, so the voice loop starts on every boot (print-only replies, no gait cap). G2 ran out of power before it could be tested after a power cycle; the BiBoard's own voice commands had also stopped responding. Resume notes: [`plan-detail/phase7-voice.md`](plan-detail/phase7-voice.md).
- **Voice → Claude → walk (2026-10-03):** wake word, speech-to-text, a Claude call and a real `walk_forward` on G2 all worked end to end (hard floor, 5 s gait cap, replies printed because there is no speaker yet). Speech recognition is the weak spot. Detail and findings: [`plan-detail/phase7-voice.md`](plan-detail/phase7-voice.md). The microphone is wired and verified ([`blueprints/biboard-pi-connector.md`](../blueprints/biboard-pi-connector.md)).
- **Milestone (2026-10-04):** with the mic and speaker installed, G2 walked, listened and talked together for the first time (wake word → speech-to-text → Claude → spoken reply → a real walk), run by the `g2-voice` boot service. That service runs `python -m pi_pipeline.voice --mode voice`, **not** the full app, and ignores the vision flags. **Vision while walking has not been tested.** Turn latency over 16 logged turns: speech-to-text wait median 1.0 s, first Claude token 1.2 s, to first move 3.1 s (p90 3.9), to first speech 3.8 s median but 9.4 s p90 (long replies; the first sentence is what matters). Spoken replies worked once the speaker was settled (see the mic/speaker row).
- **Right now (2026-10-04, until the stand is ready): G2 cannot move.** The `g2-voice` service runs with a systemd drop-in (`/etc/systemd/system/g2-voice.service.d/no-move.conf`) that swaps `--actuator serial` for `--actuator mock`. To restore movement: delete that file, `sudo systemctl daemon-reload && sudo systemctl restart g2-voice`.
- **Vision feed on the Pi (2026-10-04):** the camera feed opens on `/dev/ttyACM0` and polls cleanly with no serial link to the BiBoard; no detections in a 12 s test (nobody in view, module needs to be upright). The Pi's `.env` now has `G2_FEATURES=+vision,+vision_perception,-vision_safety,-explore,-avoidance_act,-object_gallery` (comma-separated; a bare `+vision` also turns on safety, explore and avoidance). The voice service ignores it; it takes effect only for `python -m pi_pipeline.app`. Backup of the old file: `~/bittleX/.env.bak-before-vision`.
- **Memory is live:** 36 exchanges logged on the Pi, 0 facts (G2 has not used `remember` yet). Recall works offline against a backup, but matches on single common words from garbled speech recognition ("being", "today"), so recall is noisy until recognition improves. `g2membackup` copies the DB to the Mac (first backup 2026-10-04).
- **Low-battery alert (2026-10-04):** the voice service reads G2's 2-cell pack voltage with the firmware's `P` command (real reading 7.89 V) at startup and every minute, and plays the `star_trek_whistle` once, then says "My battery is low." in `metal`, when it is **low (7.2 V or below, about 20%)**, repeating every 5 minutes, or **critical (6.6 V or below)**. An alert needs 3 consecutive low readings (the pack sags under load) and readings are skipped while a gait runs. Real voltage readings were verified on the Pi; the alert path (siren, then G2 says "My battery is low" in `metal`) is unit-tested but still has to be heard on G2 (a bug that silenced the siren was found and fixed 2026-10-04). Settings: `G2_BATTERY_WATCH`, `G2_BATTERY_LOW_V`, `G2_BATTERY_CRITICAL_V`, `G2_BATTERY_POLL_S`. The thresholds are estimates for a 2S Li-ion pack at rest (about 3.6 V per cell is 20%); adjust after seeing real discharge.  The watcher reads through its own read-only link while the actuator is mock; with the serial actuator it uses the actuator's link.
- **Pi battery runtime (2026-10-04, deployed, timed test in progress):** the PiSugar S can't report its level, so we measure how long the Pi lasts on one charge with an **intentional test** (nothing runs at startup otherwise). `python -m pi_pipeline.power runtime test start` (from a full charge, charger unplugged) starts a heartbeat; after the power dies, the next voice-service start (or `runtime test collect`) records the run. A clean shutdown or reboot during the test discards it, so a reboot is never mistaken for an empty battery. `runtime` lists runs and their mean; `runtime add <seconds>`, `runtime forget <n>`, `runtime test status|cancel`. The warning at 80% of the measured runtime (about 20% left; the `star_trek_red_alert` siren twice, then "Pi battery is low.", again at 95%) is **on but silent until a timed test completes**, and then uses only timed-test runs (the rough 1.78 h manual reading is ignored for warnings). It counts uptime since boot, so a reboot resets it. `G2_PI_BATTERY_WATCH=0` turns it off; `G2_PI_FULL_RUNTIME_S` overrides the measurement. The test now running was started from a full charge (confirmed 2026-10-04). First test: started 2026-10-04 at about 8:44 PM, right after the Pi came back from its recharge; the single earlier reading (1.78 h) is already in the log. Keep the charger unplugged and the Pi on until it dies.
- **G2 describes what he sees (2026-10-04, built and confirmed working on G2 by the user the same day):** when you ask things like "what do you see", "what are you looking at", "look around", "can you see ...", the voice loop takes one 240x240 picture (`vision/snapshot.py`, one `AT+INVOKE=1,0,0`; the 480x480 mode gets truncated by the module) and sends it to Claude with that one message, plus the on-device detector's labels as a hint. The picture is not saved and is replaced by a placeholder in the conversation history, so it is never resent. If the camera fails, Claude is told so and says so instead of guessing. `G2_VISION_DESCRIBE=0` turns it off; needs `+vision` in `G2_FEATURES` (set on the Pi). The camera opens when the wake word is heard and is released after 2 minutes idle. Not yet a tool Claude can call on his own. **Camera sounds:** a shutter chirp for every picture, a rising two-note chirp when continuous capture (the vision feed in `app`) starts, repeated every 60 s as a reminder, and a falling one when it stops; 10% level (`G2_CAMERA_SOUNDS`, `G2_CAMERA_PEAK`, `G2_CAMERA_REMINDER_S`).
- **"Sorry, I glitched" after a picture (found and fixed 2026-10-04):** the API rejected the conversation history ("Invalid `signature` in `thinking` block") because I replaced the camera picture in the history with a text placeholder after the reply; this model's short between-tool updates come back as thinking blocks, and those are cryptographically bound to everything before them. The failed turn also stayed in the history, so every later turn failed until the service restarted. Fixes (`voice/conversation.py`): a picture turn stores no thinking blocks; if the API ever rejects one, they are all stripped and the turn retried once; a failed turn is taken back out of the history and its tool acknowledgements are kept. (`thinking: {"type": "disabled"}` is not valid on this model.) **Silent look (also fixed 2026-10-04):** with a picture attached Claude sometimes answered with only a physical move (`check_around`) and no speech, so G2 said nothing; the prompt now says to always speak the description first and not to move to "look", the picture note says to describe it out loud, and the loop speaks a fallback if a picture turn still comes back silent. Replies are now logged as `reply blocks: text(n), tool_use:name`. **Mirror / self-image (2026-10-04, built):** "remember what you look like", "that's you in the mirror", "this is a mirror", "can you see yourself" take a picture and tell Claude it shows G2 himself; he describes his own appearance aloud and saves it as a memory fact ("I look like ...") with the `remember` tool. A small robot in any picture is understood to be G2. Under investigation: with a mirror, a plain "what do you see" sometimes came back with only a thinking block and `await_reply` (no spoken text), so G2 said the silent-look fallback; the log now shows the thinking text.
- **CliffGuard is wired** into the app (built whenever `vision_safety` is on; a `SensorHub` `edge_source` supplies readings) but stays inert: no floor-vs-edge classifier exists yet, so nothing feeds it.
- **Not yet run on the robot:** the full app (`python -m pi_pipeline.app --serial`), long-run reliability of spoken replies, the
  behavior runtime, and vision-based reflexes. `CliffGuard` and `Avoider` are not wired into the app (deliberately; see the plan).

## Open problems

1. **Front-left shoulder servo (servo 8) is faulty or mis-sensing:** sticks near 42° and misses its commands; the FR shoulder shows
   occasional glitch readings. Findings and ordered next steps: [`rl/real-walk-log.md`](rl/real-walk-log.md) ("Servo troubleshooting").
2. **Carpet:** every gait tried (V2.1, scripted, lift/stride variants) fails on ~1/4 in pile; falls are sideways to the right.
3. **Heading drift to the right** and a large roll swing while walking; the FR leg sags after the servos relax at rest.
4. **Standing wobble (diagnosed 2026-10-04):** G2 stands steady, then (usually after rest then stand) falls into a sustained roll/pitch oscillation of about 1.9 Hz, up to roughly ±5° in pitch, that can last a minute or more and sometimes stops on its own. It is the firmware's **gyro balance loop going unstable on a 5 Hz IMU**: with balance off (`gb`) G2 stays calm (0.1° noise) and a nudge dies out within seconds; the accelerometer (independent of the firmware's fusion) shows the same swings, battery voltage was steady (7.9 V), and the Pi was not involved. Detail: [`hardware/petoi-firmware-reference.md`](hardware/petoi-firmware-reference.md#standing-wobble-2026-10-04). **Fix built and deployed 2026-10-04 (`gait/stand_guard.py`):** firmware balance is kept **off while idle** (the voice service sends `gb` at start and every minute, and turns it on only just before a firmware gait and off just after), and a **guard** watches the 5 Hz IMU and sends `gb` if a wobble starts anyway (0.8 degrees of rhythmic swing for 4 s; tuned by replaying the five stand logs). Both pause while a gait runs. Settings: `G2_BALANCE_OFF_IDLE`, `G2_STAND_GUARD` (`G2_BALANCE_OFF_IDLE=0` leaves balance to the firmware, and the guard then re-enables it after 5 minutes of quiet, doubling the wait if the wobble returns). Not yet tested on G2 across a rest-then-stand; it needs the user to try it.
5. The scripted-vs-learned comparison (H1) is unanswered, and the 2026-10-01 walks all ran with the FL shoulder ~8° low.
