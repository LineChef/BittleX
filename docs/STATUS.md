## What is deployed

Everything below is on the Pi as of 2026-10-18 (the Pi's `pi_pipeline/.deployed_commit` names the commit).

- **Walking policy:** V7 (the name is `DEFAULT_POLICY` in `pi_pipeline/gait/residual_policy.py`), heading-blind (the Pi feeds it yaw 0), a joint command every third control tick. It is the V6 recipe retrained from scratch for 20M steps on the pod + AI head payload (sim profile `pod`, 481 g in all); the user promoted it without the benchmark rule. Against V6 on the same payload in the sim it fell less (mean cell falls 0.159 against 0.174) and climbed the lowest step-up far more often (93% against 0%); calm-walk heading is about equal (10.8 against 10.4 deg). It is the head attachment's saved gait. On hardwood (2026-10-18, 12 s walks): untrimmed 4 ft 3 in and 5 in right of centre, twice the same; with its trim `fl=-0.3`, 4 ft and 1 in right. V6, V4, V3 and V2.1 stay on the Pi as fallbacks. Training record: [`plan-detail/v6-staged-training-plan.md`](plan-detail/v6-staged-training-plan.md). Rollback: set `DEFAULT_POLICY` to the earlier file and deploy. Lineage: [`rl/hw1-log.md`](rl/hw1-log.md).
- **Steering:** the heading hold is OFF by default (`G2_FOOT_HOLD=fl` turns it on); the everyday walk uses a fixed front-left foot trim per policy (`DEFAULT_TRIMS` in `gait/heading_hold.py`: V6 and V7 `fl=-0.3`, measured on hardwood only).
- **Walk start:** after a firmware gait or skill a walk first settles into a still stand (firmware balance off, up to 2 s), and never starts while tilted past 30° (`gait/walk_start.py`).
- **Stand-ups and transitions:** every stand-up uses the eased 0.8 s ramp (`gait/standup.py`); with the arm fitted the stand-up is the plain firmware `kbalance` (the arm profile's `poses.stand_via`; easing is off, user 2026-10-25) and pose-to-pose blending is back on as its own switch (`blending`, separate from easing). Every move into a firmware skill is eased too: the serial link steps the legs from the pose it knows into the skill's first frame, slow enough that no joint peaks above 80 deg/s (the bow, look up and check are not eased at all, user 2026-10-18: eased they were too slow; they play as the firmware does, `transitions.PLAIN_SKILLS`; the survey's gaps now count from the end of each eased skill; the sim cannot flip him in any of the three poses), 40 steps a second on a fixed schedule, with firmware balance off (`link/transitions.py`). A stand-up from a sit eases down to rest first. After a firmware gait or a trick the pose is unknown and the firmware's own start is used; reading the servos instead (stage 2, `G2_TRANSITION_READ_POSE`) stays off: over the Pi's link the firmware returns no angles and the request made him lean. A stop or freeze out of a stride is immediate. On G2 (2026-10-18) the stand, sit, rest and stand-from-sit were smooth once firmware balance was held off (roll swing 2 deg against 18-25 with it on).
- **Disabled moves:** the wave, scratch, shake paw, high five and hop tip G2 over on this payload: the serial link refuses them for every caller and plays the wrong-answer horn ([`hardware/disabled-moves.md`](hardware/disabled-moves.md)). Stretch and push-ups are kept but borderline.
- **Voice:** wake word, local commands (stop, sleep, shut down, floor, "walk for N seconds", gait switching), conversation mode ("let's talk"), spoken exchange sounds. Every wake trigger and what followed it is logged to `~/.local/share/g2/wake_triggers.jsonl` (`python -m pi_pipeline.voice.wake_log`); `G2_WAKE_MIN_CONF` can ignore low-confidence triggers (off until the log shows the split). Commands: [`guides/voice-commands.md`](guides/voice-commands.md). Switches: [`guides/settings-switches.md`](guides/settings-switches.md).
- **Sleep:** on by default after 5 minutes at rest (`G2_SLEEP_AFTER_S`).
- **Memory:** live on the Pi (facts, exchange log, pinned core, sleep-time consolidation). Reflection is parked (`G2_REFLECT=off`) until there are more sessions; the recaps keep being written.
- **Exploration:** two tiers (stationary attentive, voice-armed roam) with timed turns, a wall avoider and a hit sequence (IMU contact signal). Narration says what he does and sees (walls, bumps, new directions; fixed lines, no API call). Survey pictures: four scheduled ones per capped roam (25, 50, 75 and 99 percent), plus one after a finished wall turn or a clean leg end when the cooldown allows; each is the sharpest of three frames, a picture never holds him more than 10 s, and he says when the camera is not answering. Exploration detects at the camera's picture capture option, and interest peeks pause during a picture. Place-memory logging (P1) writes one record per survey stop. Guide: [`guides/exploration-mode.md`](guides/exploration-mode.md).
- **Robustness:** the camera reader is restarted if it dies (`util/supervisor.py`); a single failed behaviour tick is skipped; every walk log records why it ended (also when the process exits) and its late-tick share; the stand guard logs a swing with balance already off as motion, not a wobble.
- **Battery alarms:** G2's pack and the Pi's runtime both warn by voice; thresholds are settings.
- **Sounds:** one at a time through an audio gate; levels in `sound_levels.py`.

## Open problems

1. **The camera stops answering during exploration** (2026-10-18: 8 timeouts of 6 s in one roam, two of four milestone pictures lost, contact pictures 7 to 9 s). Likely cause: every picture switched the capture option (detection at 480, pictures at 240) while peeks asked for pictures every few seconds. Since then exploration detects at 240 and peeks pause during a picture; not yet tested.
2. **Late walk ticks** (2026-10-18: 30 to 32% of ticks late in exploration, 477 of 488 on the tick that sends the joint command). Cause found: each streamed send waited for the serial bytes to drain. They no longer wait; walk logs now record the late-tick share. Not yet tested.
3. **A tip-over at a survey stop** (2026-10-18, 112 deg, right after a walk leg). The survey poses on this payload (look up, the bow, the check lean) are the first suspect.
4. **Wake word false triggers:** 4 of 7 triggers on 2026-10-18 were people talking nearby, each sending junk to Claude; loudness does not separate them. The trigger log is collecting data for `G2_WAKE_MIN_CONF`; `wake_log` now suggests a value once it has 3 real and 3 false triggers, and `G2_WAKE_MAX_BEFORE` can ignore a phrase buried in other talk (off until the log shows the split); saying "dismissed" after a false wake closes the window and marks it false in the log.
5. **The wall estimator needs recalibrating** for the camera's new place on the pod; its gaps (beyond 24 in only far or near; left-angle walls, the right 45° wall and corners read far) are in [`vision/wall-check-plan.md`](vision/wall-check-plan.md).
6. **The bump reflex misses thin obstacles** such as chair legs (owner, 2026-10-18): the IMU contact signal looks for a stalled walk.
7. **Speech arrives 7 to 17 s after it is decided during exploration** (2026-10-10); the policy walk sometimes "did not stop within 5 s" (six times on 2026-10-18, none in the retest). Code changes 2026-10-18, untested on G2: narration lines are cached on disk and pre-made at idle (`voice/speech_cache.py`), a line is dropped if not ready 8 s after it was decided, each line logs its queue/synthesis/play time (`narration timing:`), and a walk that does not stop logs the stack it is stuck in plus `walk.slow_end`.
8. **Heading drift on other floors.** Hardwood is handled by the trim; tile is unresolved and carpet is open.
9. **Carpet.** Every gait tried fails on about 1/4 in pile; falls are sideways to the right.
10. **BiBoard ticking** during runner-started walks: unexplained, parked by the user. First suspect if it returns: a quiet command sent every second (`G2_NOISE_LOG_ALL=1`, then `python -m pi_pipeline.link.noise_log 60`).
11. **Back-left shoulder servo** is stiffer and louder (noted 2026-10-09, parked); flag anything unusual.
12. **Stepping up and over small ledges.** No policy crosses step-ups above 7.5 mm in the sim; a high-step gait is still wanted: [`planned/README.md`](planned/README.md).
13. **Open firmware question:** "be quiet" makes the BiBoard's voice module ignore basic commands; there is no known way to remove only its spoken "ok" ([`plan-detail/phase7-voice.md`](plan-detail/phase7-voice.md)). Never send `Xa` or `XAd`.

14. **His legs go limp about 10 s after our stand-up** (2026-11-01, arm fitted; found with `claw_look`). Observed: after the test scripts stand him (`kbalance`, plain firmware stand-up) the servos lose torque 7 to 10 s later, with nothing sent and the arm not moving; sometimes he drops to a posture, sometimes unevenly (a steady 10 deg pitch and roll lean), and he can tip onto his side. It happens after the BiBoard's own voice "stand up" too (user, 2026-11-02; on 2026-11-01 it had looked as if only our stand-up did it), so it is not our stand-up path. Ruled out: the arm (`claw_look --hold` moves nothing), the thermal guard (not in these scripts), a missing `gb` (`--hold --gb` still did it), the board resetting (the IMU stream kept running), a pack voltage threshold (8.0 V and up when it happened once; the pack sags 8.24 to 7.83 V within 4 s of standing). Not yet tried: `--stand kup` (the firmware's neutral stand; built, never completed because of item 15). Tools: `python -m pi_pipeline.behavior.claw_look --hold [--stand kup] [--gb]` (stand, then watch the IMU, pack and any board text for 20 s). 2026-11-02 (ball goal, attempt #33): about 10 s standing still through two looks, then the search back-up (`kbkF`) and he fell; the user puts the falls down to the legs having gone slack while he stood. Two fixes from it: a fall during a firmware move now still sends the stop (`kbalance`; it was skipped and `kbkF` ran on for over a minute), and the judge blames a fall only on a setting whose stage was reached (it had banned punch distance 3.5 in for a fall while searching). 2026-11-02, the leading cause after online research (Petoi lists electronic overheat protection for the alloy servos; another arm owner reported legs giving way after a few seconds in one pose; the upstream firmware has no timer that releases servos): the servos' own overload protection, worst on the back legs, which carry the raised front. Being tried: in the ball chase he waits in the attention pose (`kcalib`, legs straight) instead of standing, takes each straight-ahead picture there once settled, stands up only when a move follows, and stays up only while dialing in close to the ball (the wall reading was calibrated on standing pictures and may read off from the attention pose) (`ATTENTION_WHEN_STILL` in `behavior/ball_chase.py`). First try (2026-11-02): the plain firmware `kbalance` out of the attention pose flipped him over, so a move out of it now stands him up back legs first, then the front legs (`gait/standup.stand_from_attention`, run by the link), and the claw look raises the claw straight up before panning (it sits between the front legs there) and lowers it last.
15. **Garbled serial reads right after the voice service stops** (2026-11-01): the pack read (`P`) comes back empty or as corrupted bytes, so `battery.motion_gate` fails safe ("UNREADABLE") and tests stop before moving. Seen in 7 of about 12 runs, on and off; the same reads work at other times. Checked: nothing else holds `/dev/serial0` (`fuser`), the port is the full UART (`ttyAMA0`, Bluetooth off) so it is not CPU-clock baud drift, the Pi-to-BiBoard plug was reseated once, no change. Not yet tried: a different cable, a longer wait, or reading the pack through the voice service. Later that night the board ignored every command (`P`, `j`, `gp`, `kbalance`) while still streaming IMU lines, with the heading stuck at -12002.7 deg (the 2026-10-10 overflow); after the user reseated the Pi-to-BiBoard plug the board restarted (heading 399 deg) and answered everything, and the pack read worked for the rest of the night. So check the plug (and look for a stuck heading) first. A second form, 2026-11-01 later: the board streams IMU lines nonstop (heading frozen at -806 deg) and ignores every command (`P`, `gp`, `i0 ..`) until it is power-cycled; a plug that looks seated is not the cause. The voice service starting again 16 s after a stop (a user alias) also holds the port and swallows replies: `systemctl is-active g2-voice` and `fuser /dev/ttyAMA0` before reading the board. Two traps found on the way: a stopped `g2-voice` can be restarted by a session's own wrapper and then holds the port, so check `fuser /dev/ttyAMA0` before reading the board; and `pkill -f "goals run"` also matches the ssh command that started the session. The chase's pose read (`j`) still often fails while the IMU streams, so the stand-up now runs whenever the pose cannot be read. Since 2026-11-01 (user) an unreadable pack no longer blocks a test: the gate warns and continues (a reading that works and is under 30% still stops); `G2_BATTERY_STRICT=1` restores the refusal. With no gate there is no battery protection while the voice service is stopped, so read the pack percent by voice ("what is your power level") before a test and watch him.
