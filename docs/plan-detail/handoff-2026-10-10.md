## 3. Decisions made today

- Measurements are shown to the user in inches. Battery in percent.
- No camera roll correction (a fixed correction could throw other readings off); left and right turning may be a little off at first.
- Sleep mode is on by default (5 min); sleep and the follow-up window (5 s, 8 s after a question) are things to live with, then tune.
- Nothing trains on top of a finished 20M. No gait policy training was run today. The hi-step gait is the open design question: [`high-step-gait-plan.md`](high-step-gait-plan.md).
- Later, once wall avoidance is reliable on several wall types and the planned training photos exist: most random turns off, bounce around like a robot vacuum.

## 4. Lessons (do not repeat)

- Never deploy to the Pi while an exploration session or a walk is running.
- If G2 is moving with no process in control, send rest at once: `python -m pi_pipeline.link.check_serial send d` on the Pi.
- A sound's `peak` is a FRACTION of full scale (0.0225); passing a large number clips at full volume.
- A command chain with `;` does not stop on a failing test: use `&&` before committing.
- Piper reads "G2" as a soft g; a hard-g spelling was misheard by the recognizer as "the to". The name is spoken as "gee two".

## 6. Plan for the next hardware session (about 2.5 h; the Pi lasts about 3.3 h on a full charge, so start both packs full and stop before the Pi's low alarm at 2.3 h)

**Approved 2026-10-10 (user): Blocks 0 and A to F; tests on his feet (no holding him in the hand). Block G (our own teach tool, building a new hi-step gait) is added at the END of the list, only if there is time.**

**Before G2 is touched (10 min).** Charge both packs. Dry-run the deploy to confirm the Pi matches the repo (section 1), and never deploy once a session has started. Floor label `hardwood`. Have ready: a tape measure and two tape lines on the floor (straight at the wall, and one at 45 degrees to each side), a book or other block for the stall tests, a box, a door and a chair if they can be used. Write down the G2 pack percent and the Pi's uptime at the start and the end of every block.

**Block A, sounds and safety, voice service running (15 min).**
1. Say "G2, switch to English" is not needed; just check "G2 online." sounds like "gee two" after a restart.
2. Fall horn: with your hands on him, tip him gently onto his side 3 times for a second each. Expect the losing horn and a `G2 is tipped over` line in `journalctl -u g2-voice`. If it does not play, read that journal first.
3. Sleep: leave him at rest for 5 minutes, then wake him with "gee two" (sigh, then yawn). Note how long the wake took.
4. Follow-up window: ask a question and answer inside 5 s without the wake word; note whether the closing notes play.

**Block B, IMU tests, voice service stopped (25 min).** Goal: decide whether an IMU contact signal is worth building. All on the floor, on his feet or at rest; no stand, no held-in-the-air test.
1. Rest capture: `python -m pi_pipeline.gait.imu_capture --seconds 60 --pose rest`. Record the frame rate (expect 5 Hz), the accel bias and noise per axis, and the roll and pitch noise.
2. Stand capture: the same with `--pose stand`. Compare with rest (the gravity split between axes tells how the accel is mounted).
3. Stall captures, 3 runs: a book or wall about 8 in in front of him, `python pi_pipeline/gait/run_gait.py --cmd 0.10 --seconds 8 --log ~/g2_runs/imu_stall_N.csv` (hands near; stop at once if he climbs the book). The log carries the IMU frames. Look in the CSV for what changes when the legs keep stepping but he does not move: pitch and roll mean shift, the step-frequency vibration in the accel, a drop in forward accel variance.
4. Free-walk runs for tuning, 3 runs: a measured 6 ft lane on hardwood, `run_gait.py --cmd 0.10 --seconds 20 --log ~/g2_runs/imu_walk_N.csv`; note the true distance from the tape. These let the ZUPT speed estimate be checked offline against a known speed.
5. Pull the logs and run `tools/walk_log_summary.py`; compare stall against free walk. Decision rule: build an IMU stall detector only if the stall runs separate from the walk runs by a margin that holds on all 3 of each; otherwise the vision signals stay the only contact signal.

**Block C, wall pictures (25 min, voice service stopped).** Use `python -m pi_pipeline.vision.wall_pictures shot --label ... --inches ...` for every shot (it waits for stillness, warms the camera, retakes a bad exposure). Straight-on wall at 8, 12, 16, 24 and 40 in (twice each, picking G2 up and putting him down between), far wall at 6 and 10 ft, wall at 45 degrees right and left from the tape lines, an inside corner at 16 in, a door, a rug edge, a chair leg, the box, a mirror or window. About 25 pictures. Then rebuild the calibration (`wall_distance pictures DIR ...`), run `wall_replay DIR`, and compare with prototype set 1 (exposure, repeatability, the left angle).

**Block D, avoider tests with a person next to him (30 min).** `bash tools/g2_explore.sh start 90`, G2 on hardwood in a cleared area, from about 6 ft, 2 ft and 1 ft out. Runs: straight wall (3), wall at each angle (2 each), corner (2), a hit from 2 ft to see the oof, back up and turn around (3), each wall type you have (painted, door, panelling). For each run note: did he turn, at what distance (inches, estimate by eye), was the turn-away sound heard, did he touch the wall, any circling. Success: at least 8 of 10 straight-wall runs turn away with at least 4 in to spare; the hit sequence finishes without circling. Afterwards `python -m pi_pipeline.vision.wall_stats` and `wall_replay` on the saved near-wall pictures.

**Block E, camera in a roam (10 min).** One exploration with a picture stop. In the saved `.json` files check `still: true`, `retaken`, and the exposure mean (85 to 140). Review them in `g2pics`.

**Block F, exploration with more wall types and the recognition photos (as time allows).** A longer roam (4 to 5 min) in a second room if possible, then Block 6 of the hardware plan: label the first 5 pictures per object (plan [`../vision/exploration-object-learning-plan.md`](../vision/exploration-object-learning-plan.md)).

**Block G, last and only if time allows: test our own skill composer (`teach.py`) and build a new hi-step gait with it (30 min, voice service stopped).** `python -m pi_pipeline.gait.teach --out ~/g2_taught/hi_step.json --leg fl` on the Pi. First `check` (stands, reads the servos back, compares with the balance pose) and `raw` (the reply format of `f` and `j` is undocumented); only if `check` says the mapping looks right, use `hand` / `grab`. Then teach one front leg round a step in 4 to 6 steps (stance, lift, high point, reach, set down), either by hand (`hand`, move the leg, `grab`, `save`) or by jog (`fl knee +5`); `play` runs the steps back on G2. Copy the JSON to the Mac and run `build_taught_step.py`, check it with `gait_probe.py` before G2 walks on it.

**Rules for the session.** Hands near him; rest him before stopping anything that controls him (`bash tools/g2_safe_stop.sh voice|explore`); if he moves with nothing in control, `python -m pi_pipeline.link.check_serial send d` on the Pi; never deploy during an exploration or a walk; one change per test; the user says "go" before each run and measures with a tape in inches; abort a block if the G2 pack passes 30% (resting) or the Pi's low alarm sounds.
