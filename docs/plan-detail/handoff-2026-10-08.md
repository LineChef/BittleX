## 1. Steering and drift (hardware, 2026-10-07)

- **The front feet steer G2.** About 9-10 deg/s of turn per unit of front-foot trim; the back feet do not steer. Longer right strides
  turn G2 left (opposite of the sim).
- **Front-left foot hold** (`pi_pipeline/gait/heading_hold.py`, `FootHold`): P (kp 0.02, 6 deg deadband) + rate term (kd 0.08) +
  integral (ki 0.01, limit 0.4); output clamped to [-0.6, +0.2]; slews up at 0.3/s, releases at 1.2/s once heading is at or below
  target or G2 is already turning left faster than 3 deg/s. Feed-forward (`G2_FOOT_HOLD_FF`) defaults to 0: the A/B showed no benefit.
  `G2_FOOT_HOLD=fl` is the default for every policy walk (`default_foot_hold()`); `--foot-hold off` gives a control.
- **Results:** V2.1 for 25 s with no hold turned +148 deg; with the hold +13 to +21 deg. On a tape line the held walks stayed within
  about 15 cm over 6 ft, even though the IMU yaw read +20 to +33 deg (an unexplained offset or crab; logged, not resolved).
- **Tile:** with the hold, 2 falls in 4 walks (at 15 s and 11.5 s). The user's call: keep the hold on everywhere, the same foot-catch
  fall has happened on tile before. Count tile falls from logs as walking continues.
- **Rules from the user:** all walks on real hardware use the drift correction; controlled (held) runs only, no uncontrolled long
  walks; hold data is never fed to the sim.
- **Weighing:** front legs 171 g and rear 210 g; re-weigh 185 g and 206 g (about 46% front). Sim default was 44.4% front.

## 2. BiBoard noises

- **Idle tick** (about once a minute): isolation tests showed the Pi's battery read `P` and the stand guard `gb` each make the board
  tick. Fixed by reading the battery on demand (start, wake word, before a walk) plus a 30 min backstop (`G2_BATTERY_POLL_S` 1800) and
  re-asserting stand every 10 min (`G2_STAND_REASSERT_S` 600). The alarms still fire because the battery is read before each walk.
- **Rapid ticking during runner-started walks** (not voice-started): unexplained; `P` every 60 s did not stop it. Parked (user), no
  reflash. Check here first if it comes back.
- **Chirps** (`pi_pipeline/behavior/chirps.py`) now play in a lower register (heard on G2). Every command that makes the board sound is
  logged to `~/.local/share/g2/noise.jsonl` (`python -m pi_pipeline.link.noise_log [N]`; `G2_NOISE_LOG_ALL=1` also logs quiet
  commands). Test with `python -m pi_pipeline.behavior.chirp_test` (the BiBoard must be on).

5. **Noise log flood:** the quiet-command filter skips `i ` and `m` but joint streams look like `i8 36 12 ...` (no space), so

## 9. Voice service fixes later on 2026-10-08 (after the sections above)

Confirmed working by the user at about 9:55 AM after the last deploy. What was wrong and what changed:

- **Segfault when a command window timed out.** The crash report (`diag/crashwatch.py`, `python -m pi_pipeline.diag.crashwatch`) caught it: `sounddevice close()` on the handed-over microphone stream crashed PortAudio, 55 s into listening. Fix: one microphone stream for the life of the process, never closed (`voice/wake_word.py`, bounded queue, wake detector and speech recogniser take turns reading it). Follow-up listening windows must use the same stream (`hand_over()` always returns it): opening a second stream fails with "Device unavailable", which restarted the service after every picture.
- **The microphone is very quiet.** Measured with `python -m pi_pipeline.voice.check_audio mic 15` (stop g2-voice first): speech at 10 cm is about 20-55 RMS with a constant offset near -900, 20-50 times too quiet. The Google voiceHAT card has no mixer controls, so `voice/mic_gain.py` removes the offset and applies x20 gain (limited, not clipped; `G2_MIC_GAIN`).
- **G2 heard himself.** The spoken Pi-battery alarm triggered the wake word ("hey buddy") and became a command ("hi battery is low"). The wake detector and the command window now ignore audio while `tts.SPEAKING` is set and for 0.8 s after.
- **A battery read at the wake word was removed:** every `P` makes the BiBoard tick, which sounded like the board answering. Readings are taken before each walk and on the 30 min backstop.
- **Alarm thresholds (Pi `.env`, 2026-10-08):** G2 pack low 7.75 V (about 35% left, interpolated; critical 7.4 V default); Pi battery warns at 65% of runtime used (`G2_PI_WARN_FRACTION`).
- **systemd:** the voice unit restarts 1 s after a failure (at most 10 times in 60 s) and runs with faulthandler on.
- **Unresolved:** a movement and fall right after a wake word earlier in the morning. The Pi sent no motion command at that time, so the BiBoard's own voice module is the suspect; it did not recur after the fixes. The `XAd` rule still stands.
- **Lesson:** do not deploy (which restarts the voice service) while the user is testing; two of their attempts were cut off that way.
- **Also today:** scheduled curation of the exploration pictures (`tools/g2_curate_auto.sh`, every 30 min, output `training_data/exploration/auto`); `g2pics` tab counts and the Labeled / Not labeled yet layout; two throwaway camera frames after the capture-mode switch for steadier exposure.
