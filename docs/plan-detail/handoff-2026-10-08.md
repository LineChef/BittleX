A dated record of what was learned, decided, built and left open across the long hardware and V3 session. Current truth lives in

Times are Eastern.

## 1. Steering and drift (hardware, 2026-10-07)

- **The front feet steer G2.** About 9-10 deg/s of turn per unit of front-foot trim; the back feet do not steer. Longer right strides
  turn G2 left (opposite of the sim).
- **Front-left foot hold** (`pi_pipeline/gait/heading_hold.py`, `FootHold`): P (kp 0.02, 6 deg deadband) + rate term (kd 0.08) +
  integral (ki 0.01, limit 0.4); output clamped to [-0.6, +0.2]; slews up at 0.3/s, releases at 1.2/s once heading is at or below
  target or G2 is already turning left faster than 3 deg/s. Feed-forward (`G2_FOOT_HOLD_FF`) defaults to 0: the A/B showed no benefit.
  `G2_FOOT_HOLD=fl` is the default for every policy walk (`default_foot_hold()`); `--foot-hold off` gives a control.
- **Results:** V2.1 for 25 s with no hold turned +148 deg; with the hold +13 to +21 deg. On a tape line the held walks stayed within
  about 15 cm over 6 ft, even though the IMU yaw read +20 to +33 deg (an unexplained offset or crab; logged, not resolved).
- **Tile:** with the hold, 2 falls in 4 walks (at 15 s and 11.5 s). The user's call: keep the hold on everywhere, the same foot-catch
  fall has happened on tile before. Count tile falls from logs as walking continues.
- **Rules from the user:** all walks on real hardware use the drift correction; controlled (held) runs only, no uncontrolled long
  walks; hold data is never fed to the sim.
- **Weighing:** front legs 171 g and rear 210 g; re-weigh 185 g and 206 g (about 46% front). Sim default was 44.4% front.

## 2. BiBoard noises

- **Idle tick** (about once a minute): isolation tests showed the Pi's battery read `P` and the stand guard `gb` each make the board
  tick. Fixed by reading the battery on demand (start, wake word, before a walk) plus a 30 min backstop (`G2_BATTERY_POLL_S` 1800) and
  re-asserting stand every 10 min (`G2_STAND_REASSERT_S` 600). The alarms still fire because the battery is read before each walk.
- **Rapid ticking during runner-started walks** (not voice-started): unexplained; `P` every 60 s did not stop it. Parked (user), no
  reflash. Check here first if it comes back.
- **Chirps** (`pi_pipeline/behavior/chirps.py`) now play in a lower register (heard on G2). Every command that makes the board sound is
  logged to `~/.local/share/g2/noise.jsonl` (`python -m pi_pipeline.link.noise_log [N]`; `G2_NOISE_LOG_ALL=1` also logs quiet
  commands). Test with `python -m pi_pipeline.behavior.chirp_test` (the BiBoard must be on).

## 4. Recognition (built, not wired)

`vision/embedder.py`, `localizer.py`, `recognizer.py`, `ObjectGallery.match()`, `tools/eval_embedder.py` (+ `--sweep`). Models in
`~/g2_data/models/` (not in the repo); sources, sizes and hashes in [`../vision/downloaded-models.md`](../vision/downloaded-models.md).
On the Pi: MobileNetV2 181 ms / 76 MB; DINOv2 small 2.3 s / 183 MB (7.7 s load). Evaluation on 4 dishwasher + 5 lamp pictures plus 23
survey pictures: DINOv2 had 0% wrong names and the best gap (0.23), the histogram embedder 22% wrong, MobileNetV2 unusable. Survey
pictures have no ground truth (many probably contain named objects), so false-recognition numbers are unreliable. Next: the user names

recognition. Wiring into exploration (phase 5) has not started.

## 5. Exploration (the open problem, 2026-10-08 morning)

Built: turn-to-bearing (timed turns at measured firmware rates: left 11, right 18 deg/s, gain 0.85), walk up to novel things
(`approach_novelty` on), "cancel exploration" words, "Exploration completed." then rest, `listen_hold` (silence the explorer while a
command window is open), tight command grammar recognizer alongside the full Vosk model.

**Still broken on the morning test (session pid 8024, 8:34-8:37 AM):**
1. **Stop words are not recognized.** The wake word is heard, an 8 s command window opens, and every time the log says "nothing
   recognized after the wake word" (then "I didn't catch that."). Suspects, in order: narration (slow Piper, 4-19 s lag) still talking
   or queued over the window; servo and walking noise (survey choreography and walks continued through the window, `listen_hold` only
   holds the explorer); mic level. Next step: log the STT hypotheses and mic level during the window, mute and drop narration while it
   is open, and pause choreographies too.
2. **The robot does not acknowledge the wake word** during exploration (user, 8:38 AM). The wake chime (`G2_CUE_STAGES=awake`) is
   wired in the voice loop; check whether the explore listener plays it at all.
3. **Twitching at rest after a halt.** After a fall the session halts and `EmergencyStop` re-sends rest (`d`) every 5 s; the user sees
   twitching. Happened twice (once at the first session end, again at 8:37:20 AM when the user turned G2 upside down to stop exploration because the wake word was not acknowledged; the fall guard read tilt 179 deg and halted). Flipping G2 is the user's only stop while the voice path is dead, so a working stop is a priority. Ending the session fixes it
   (`bash tools/g2_explore.sh stop`). The de-dup in `WalkerSink.stop()` (`last_motion_command`) is not effective live: `ImuFanout`
   (`link/fanout.py`) wraps the link and does not expose `last_motion_command`, so the sink may be seeing a wrapper. Fix: expose it
   through the wrapper, or track resting state inside the sink; and send rest once while halted, not every 5 s.
4. **Narration is late.** "Why isn't he narrating?" Lines are generated (about 17 per session) but spoken 4-19 s later on the loaded
   Pi Zero 2 W, and dropped when the queue is full. Consider shorter or pre-rendered phrases.
5. **Noise log flood:** the quiet-command filter skips `i ` and `m` but joint streams look like `i8 36 12 ...` (no space), so
   `G2_NOISE_LOG_ALL=1` fills the log. Filter on `i` followed by a digit.
6. Earlier: a segfault 4.5 min in (Pi had 28 MB free) is still untraced; falls on his back happen in exploration.

Rules in force: never deploy while an exploration session runs; the DINOv2 timing tests also stopped `g2-voice` during the user's
session, so tell the user before using the Pi.
